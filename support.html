Premium

  • Restore a purchase: More › Settings › Restore purchases, on a device signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel the monthly plan: Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

To change your ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland), use More › Settings › Ad privacy choices. See our privacy policy.

How the estimates work

The App uses Canadian conventions: fixed rates compounded twice a year, variable rates monthly, mortgage default insurance below 20% down, minimum down payment rules and the stress test. Full details are on the methodology page.
